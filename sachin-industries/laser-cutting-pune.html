Fiber laser cutting.
Built around your part.

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

What is your laser cutting bed size?+

Our fiber laser has a working bed of 8,000 × 2,500 mm.

How thick can your fiber laser cut?+

Our stated maximum is up to 40 mm. The achievable thickness and cut quality depend on the material grade and part requirements.

Do you laser cut mild steel and stainless steel?+

We welcome mild steel and stainless steel enquiries. Send the grade, thickness and drawing to confirm suitability.
