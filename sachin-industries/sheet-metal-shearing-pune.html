THE DETAILS

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

What is the maximum shearing capacity?+

Our stated maximums are up to 25 mm thickness and 8 metres length. The allowable combination depends on the material and job requirements.

Should my part be sheared or laser cut?+

Shearing suits straight cuts and preparing blanks. Laser cutting accommodates complex profiles and cut-outs. Share your drawing and we can discuss the appropriate process.

What details do you need for a shearing enquiry?+

Please include material grade, thickness, cut dimensions, quantity and target date, plus any edge or tolerance requirements.
