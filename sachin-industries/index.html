07 / GOOD QUESTIONS

Before
we make it.

What sheet metal services do you provide?+

We provide fiber laser cutting, sheet metal bending and sheet metal shearing at our Bhosari, Pune factory. Share your drawings so we can confirm which processes your components require.

How large a sheet can you laser cut?+

Our 12 kW fiber laser has an 8,000 × 2,500 mm bed and a maximum cutting thickness of up to 40 mm. Actual cutting suitability depends on the metal grade, thickness and part geometry.

Can you bend and shear heavy, long plates?+

Our headline bending and shearing capacities are up to 25 mm thickness and up to 8 metres in length. Please send your material grade and drawing; maximum thickness and maximum length are not a guarantee for every combination.

What should I send for a quotation?+

Share the drawing or part file, material grade, thickness, overall dimensions, quantity, required operations and target date. Email your files to sachinindustries1660@gmail.com or discuss the job by phone.

Where is your factory?+

Find Sachin Industries at S-68, MIDC, Bhosari, Pimpri-Chinchwad, Pune 411026, Maharashtra, India. Contact us to arrange a visit and discuss your project.
