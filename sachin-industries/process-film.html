Every process.
A different purpose.

00:00–00:08 / LASER CUTTING

Focused power defines the profile.

The film first reveals a large-format laser machine, then moves to an illustrated cutting head tracing a line into a metal sheet. Sachin Industries has a 12 kW fiber laser with an 8,000 × 2,500 mm bed and headline cutting capacity up to 40 mm.

Explore fiber laser cutting in Pune ↗

00:08–00:16 / BENDING

Flat material takes its form.

A press brake moves into a close-up of a punch, a V die and a metal sheet. The punch descends, forms the bend and retracts while the sheet keeps its new shape. Our stated bending capability reaches up to 25 mm thickness and 8 metres in length, subject to material, tooling and part geometry.

Explore sheet metal bending ↗

00:16–00:24 / SHEARING

A straight cut starts the next step.

The shearing machine gives way to an illustrated blade and fixed edge. The moving blade passes the sheet and the separated strip drops away. Our headline shearing capacities are up to 25 mm thickness and 8 metres in length, subject to the material and cut requirements.

Explore heavy-duty shearing ↗
