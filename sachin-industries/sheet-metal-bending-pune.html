Sheet metal bending.
Built around your part.

A successful bend begins before the press comes down. Our experienced team considers the material, geometry and sequence needed to turn flat sheet into useful form. With headline capability up to eight metres long, we take on substantial bending work.

MAXIMUM THICKNESS*
25 mm
MAXIMUM LENGTH*
8,000 mm
PROCESS

*Bending capacity depends on material strength, bend length, tooling, required radius and part geometry. Maximum length and maximum thickness cannot be assumed to apply together. Please send your drawing for an assessment.

THE DETAILS

GOOD
QUESTIONS.

What is your maximum bending length?+

Our headline maximum bending length is 8 metres. The applicable length depends on the material, thickness, tooling and bend geometry.

Can you bend 25 mm plate?+

Our stated capacity is up to 25 mm, subject to material, bend length and tooling. We confirm each job from its drawing.

Can you laser cut and then bend a part?+

Yes, we offer both laser cutting and bending at our factory. Send the full drawing so we can review the combined process.
