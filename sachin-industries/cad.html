Your idea.
Your drawing. Your file.

A free online 2D CAD editor from Sachin Industries, built for laser-cut profiles, bend-line layouts, straight-cut blanks and everyday drafting. Open it whenever you need to turn an idea into precise geometry.

01 / DRAW WITH INTENT

Precision comes first.

Lines, polylines, three-point arcs, circles, ellipses, polygons, slots and cubic splines. Enter absolute, relative or polar coordinates, use object snaps, and inspect geometry numerically.

02 / MAKE THE EDIT

More than a sketchpad.

Move, copy, rotate, mirror, scale, align, offset, trim, extend, fillet, chamfer, join and explode. Add rectangular or polar arrays, combine closed profiles and manage visible, locked layers.

03 / TAKE YOUR WORK

Open files. Open possibilities.

Import ASCII DXF. Export DXF or SVG, download an editable project, or print a drawing. Cut, bend, annotation and construction layers keep the purpose of each line clear.

04 / THINK IN SHEET METAL

From flat to fabrication.

Generate mounting plates, slotted profiles, shearing blanks and single-bend flat layouts. Estimate bend allowance from your own radius, thickness and K-factor, then review your drawing with the factory.

GOOD TO KNOW

Designed to be useful.

Can anyone use Sachin CAD?

Yes. The editor is free to use without an account or a quotation request. You can download your drawings and use them with other compatible software.

Are my drawings uploaded to Sachin Industries?

No. Drawing, import, export and automatic recovery happen on your device. Save a project file for a durable copy. To request fabrication, export your drawing and attach it to an email yourself.

Can I export DXF for laser cutting?

Yes. Export visible geometry as DXF in millimetres or inches, with an option for cut and bend geometry only. Lines, circles, arcs, polylines with bulges and full ellipses use native DXF entities. Edited Bezier curves use cubic SPLINE segments. Confirm units, contour closure and process requirements with your fabricator.

Does the bend calculator guarantee the final part?

No. It estimates one bend using the outside leg dimensions, bend angle, inside radius, thickness and K-factor you provide. Material behaviour, tooling, grain direction and springback require shop verification.
