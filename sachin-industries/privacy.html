YOUR PRIVACY.

A simple brochure website.

This brochure website has no visitor accounts. Interactive 3D views run in your browser. When Google Analytics is configured, it loads only after you accept analytics. You can decline optional analytics and continue using every service and enquiry feature.

Optional analytics.

With consent, Google Analytics 4 measures page visits, service interest, contact-link clicks, enquiry preparation, brochure interactions and website performance. We do not send enquiry names, company names, drawings or message text to analytics. Advertising storage, Google signals and advertising personalisation are disabled. Analytics cookies may be set by Google after acceptance. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change your choice; withdrawal disables further analytics and removes accessible Google Analytics cookies. Your preference is saved in this browser’s local storage.

Project enquiries.

Details typed into the enquiry form are held in the current page only. Preparing an email or WhatsApp message opens the chosen app with your enquiry. You decide whether to send it. Information you send to Sachin Industries is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the requested work.

Local CAD drawings.

Sachin CAD processes drawing, import and export on your device. Local recovery is stored in your browser. Files, drawing names, geometry and coordinates are not uploaded. Download a project for a lasting copy. Optional consent-controlled analytics records tool visits and file-format choices without drawing contents.

External services.

Links to Google Maps, WhatsApp and your email application are subject to those services’ privacy practices. The embedded factory map contacts Google only after you choose to load it. Google then receives technical connection information and applies its own privacy practices. The hosting provider may process technical request information, including IP addresses, to deliver and secure this website.
