SACHIN INDUSTRIES / A BUYER’S GUIDE

From sheet metal
to your next component.

Planning a metal fabrication project? This practical guide explains laser cutting, bending and shearing: what each process does, which details matter, and what to send when you need sheet metal work in Pune.

Discuss your drawing ↗

One material.
Three different jobs.

Laser cutting: define the profile.

Choose laser cutting for a shaped outline, holes, slots and detailed cut-outs. Our 12 kW fiber laser has an 8,000 × 2,500 mm bed and a headline cutting capacity up to 40 mm. Material grade, feature size, thickness and edge requirements determine suitability.

Bending: give the sheet its form.

Bending creates angled and folded shapes from flat material. Sachin Industries has headline capability up to 25 mm thickness and 8 metres in length. The applicable combination depends on material strength, bend length, radius, tooling and part geometry.

Shearing: cut straight to size.

Shearing suits straight-line cutting, strips and rectangular blanks. Our stated maximums are up to 25 mm thickness and 8 metres in length, subject to material and job assessment. For a part with complex cut-outs, discuss laser cutting instead.

A big number
needs the right context.

Maximum thickness and maximum length describe headline capabilities. They do not mean every metal grade can be processed at both maximums simultaneously. For bending, the required angle and radius also matter. For laser cutting, very small features in thick plate need particular review.

Send the actual part drawing and material specification. Our team will confirm whether the job suits the process, the applicable capacity and the scope before production.

What to send
for a useful quotation.

  • A legible drawing or part file showing the finished component.
  • Material type and grade, such as the specific mild steel or stainless steel grade.
  • Sheet or plate thickness, overall dimensions and required quantity.
  • Required operations: laser cutting, shearing, bending, or a combination.
  • Bend angles, radii and important dimensions where applicable.
  • Any edge, finish or tolerance requirements.
  • Your target date and collection or transport requirements.

If your project also requires welding, surface treatment or assembly, include those requirements so the team can confirm the scope. Don’t assume an additional operation is included in a sheet processing quotation.
